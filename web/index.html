AevumPay — pay-links for agents

One end-to-end tUSD8 payment flow on Aevum testnet (chain 44005). A merchant creates a payment link; a buyer pays it once — either the standard way (approve + pay) or gaslessly: the buyer signs an EIP-3009 authorization off-chain and anyone relays it. Agents can register a human-readable name (Confirmation of Payee).
